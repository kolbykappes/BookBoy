## Premise & scope

First-person co-op organizing/cleaning sim. Story hook: you play the Alchemist's homunculi, who
tried to cheat by casting a cleaning spell that turned into a rogue tornado spell, trashing
thousands of items across **three zones**. You chase down your own creation and clean up its mess
before your creator returns. Sort wands, tarot decks, and potions to earn points and unlock spells.

## Mechanics (as announced)

Same shape as `Shelves and Sorcery` (magic-shop setting, ability unlocks) but with an explicit
comedic framing device (your own magic went wrong) rather than a generic "shop got ransacked"
premise.

## Relevance to BookBoy

- This is the **second** magic-shop/library-adjacent entrant after `Shelves and Sorcery`
  (BookBoy would be a third, in a library rather than shop). Confirms the "magical retail" theme
  specifically — not just cozy sorting broadly — is being actively mined by multiple
  developers right now. Worth watching how differentiated it manages to feel from
  `Shelves and Sorcery` once it ships, since BookBoy sits in the same thematic neighborhood.
