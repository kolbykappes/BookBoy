## Premise & scope

Post-Black-Friday mall cleanup: **over 30,000 things to tidy** — misplaced products to return,
rubbish to collect, dirt to scrub, fallen furniture to set back upright. The store page explicitly
warns players not to expect to finish it in one sitting.

## Mechanics

Broader than pure shelving — combines put-away sorting with cleaning tasks (scrubbing, righting
furniture), a wider task variety than most of this set.

## Relevance to BookBoy

- The most extreme example yet of leading with raw scale as the pitch — 30,000+ items is roughly
  10x `Librarian`'s 3,072 books, and the marketing explicitly frames "you won't finish this" as a
  feature. This is the clearest real-world instance of the "object count" trap called out in
  [01-vision-and-design.md](../01-vision-and-design.md) — worth revisiting after launch to see
  whether reviews find that framing satisfying or exhausting.
- Its mixed task types (sort + clean + repair-adjacent righting furniture) is a mild data point
  in favor of BookBoy's later "clean, repair, translate, authenticate" mechanics list, though at a
  much larger and less curated scale than BookBoy intends.
