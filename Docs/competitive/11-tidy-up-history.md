## Premise & scope

Inherit a chaotic museum. Tidy messy rooms, unlock abilities, find hidden keys to open new wings.
The stated goal is to **uncover historical knowledge to properly identify and showcase artifacts**
— not just put things away, but figure out what they are first.

## Mechanics (as announced)

Identify-then-display framing rather than pure color-match sorting — the closest philosophical
match to BookBoy's own long-term "restoration" framing
([01-vision-and-design.md](../01-vision-and-design.md): "not simply putting items away, but
determining what they are, restoring them, and deciding where they belong").

## Relevance to BookBoy

- This is the title to watch most closely once it ships: if "identify, then place" resonates with
  players here, it's direct evidence for BookBoy's stated long-term differentiation strategy
  (classification/discovery/curation over raw sorting volume). If it flops, that's useful signal
  too — revisit this doc post-launch with review data.
