# DinoBones: Tidy Up the Museum

- **Steam:** [store.steampowered.com/app/5064340](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5064340/DinoBones_Tidy_Up_the_Museum/) (App ID 5064340)
- **Releases:** ~September 22, 2026 (planned — "unlocks in ~2 weeks" as of 2026-09-06)
- **Price:** Not yet listed
- **Reviews (2026-09-06):** None yet (unreleased)

## Premise & scope — assembly, not just shelving

Cozy dinosaur-skeleton restoration sim: collect bones scattered across the museum floor, identify
which specific dinosaur each piece belongs to, and assemble each one into a complete skeleton.
6+ dinosaur skeletons from 2,500+ bones. No time limits or penalties. The store page explicitly
states no AI-generated content was used in gameplay (a positioning choice echoed by
`Shelve the Potions!`).

Per the Steam news hub, the museum itself has already been "rebuilt from the ground up" pre-launch
for a larger, more comfortable space — suggests active iteration even before release.

## Mechanics (as announced)

- **Identify, then assemble** rather than identify-then-place-on-a-shelf — closer to a fitting/
  matching puzzle (which bone goes on which skeleton, and where on that skeleton) than a pure
  sort. Along with `Shelve the Potions!` and `Tidy Up History`, this is one of the few entrants
  clearly doing something mechanically distinct from "match color to shelf."

## Relevance to BookBoy

- Strongest evidence yet that "identify what this object actually is" (not just "what color bin
  does it go in") is an emerging, well-liked sub-mechanic in this genre. Directly supports
  BookBoy's long-term direction toward symbol/series identification over pure color sorting
  ([01-vision-and-design.md](../01-vision-and-design.md)).
- The "no AI-generated content" callout appearing across multiple unrelated teams in this genre is
  worth noting as an audience-expectation signal, independent of whatever tools are used in
  BookBoy's own development pipeline internally.
