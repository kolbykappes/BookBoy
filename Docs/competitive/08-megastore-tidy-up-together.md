# Megastore: Tidy Up Together

- **Steam:** [store.steampowered.com/app/5027520](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5027520/Megastore_Tidy_Up_Together/) (App ID 5027520)
- **Released:** September 2, 2026
- **Price:** $5.99 (introductory $5.09 through Sep 16)
- **Reviews (2026-09-06):** Very Positive — 150 total, 88.0% positive (132 pos / 18 neg)
- **Developer:** Wudderda

## Premise & scope

Cozy solo-or-co-op organizing across three stores: a Toy Store (3,555 items across 297 shelves), a
supermarket, and a two-story Megastore. Teams can split by floor, department, or shelf.

## Mechanics

- Pure visual sort, three escalating store sizes.
- Roadmap disclosed at launch: two more stores in development, plus a promised **custom level
  creation** feature in a future major update — the only title in this set announcing
  user-generated-content plans.

## Relevance to BookBoy

- The disclosed custom-level-creation roadmap is worth watching: if user-generated content proves
  popular here, it validates that this audience wants more than passive consumption of
  pre-built rooms — relevant context if BookBoy ever considers community content post-MVP (not a
  near-term concern per [02-mvp-spec.md](../02-mvp-spec.md)).
- Shares the retail-reskin, co-op-first shape of `Mall` and `Supermarket Chaos` — reinforces that
  this is the dominant, most crowded sub-lane of the genre, not the one BookBoy is aiming at.
